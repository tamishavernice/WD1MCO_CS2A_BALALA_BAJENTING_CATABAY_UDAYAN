Synopsis

Queen Elsa of Arendelle begins hearing a mysterious voice calling to her from far away. When she answers it with the song "Into the Unknown," she accidentally awakens the ancient spirits of the Enchanted Forest. Arendelle is struck by disasters, and the kingdom is forced to evacuate.

To find the cause, Elsa sets out with her sister Anna, Kristoff, Sven and Olaf. They travel north to the Enchanted Forest, which is sealed behind a wall of mist, and find the Northuldra people and a group of Arendellian soldiers who have been trapped there for decades.

They learn that the conflict began when a dam, a "gift" from Elsa and Anna's grandfather, King Runeard, to the Northuldra, ended in a fight that killed him. The spirits of the forest (Wind, Fire, Water and Earth) grew angry and sealed the forest in mist. Elsa discovers that her mother, Queen Iduna, was a Northuldra woman who once saved their father, Agnarr, from that fight.

Elsa follows the voice to Ahtohallan, a legendary glacier that holds the memories of the past. There she learns the truth about the dam and about herself: she is the "fifth spirit," the bridge between humans and nature. But the journey costs her: Elsa freezes in Ahtohallan, and Olaf fades away, which makes Anna believe her sister is gone.

Heartbroken but determined to do "the next right thing," Anna destroys the dam to set the forest free. The flood heads for Arendelle until Elsa, thawed, holds back the wave. The mist lifts, the Northuldra and Arendelle are at peace, Olaf returns, Anna becomes Queen of Arendelle, and Elsa chooses to stay in the Enchanted Forest as its protector.
